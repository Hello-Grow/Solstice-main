# Solstice — Steam Store Description

## Short Description

An atmospheric 2.5D narrative adventure about finding meaning in the final five minutes of the world. Explore, talk to your neighbors, play arcade games, and decide how to spend the sunset.

## About This Game

The world is ending in five minutes.

You have one last evening to spend however you want. Leave your apartment and explore a quiet neighborhood as the sun slowly sets. Talk to the people around you, listen to what they are carrying, and help them make their final moments a little brighter.

Solstice is a short, single-player narrative adventure focused on exploration, conversation, and small acts of kindness. There is no grand quest or world-saving mission. Your goal is simply to decide what matters to you before time runs out.

### Features

- **A focused five-minute experience** — A real-time countdown carries the story from afternoon light to sunset and the end of the world.
- **Explore a stylized 2.5D neighborhood** — Walk through your apartment, visit the local arcade, and spend the final moments in the park.
- **Meet a cast of memorable characters** — Have choice-driven conversations with neighbors, arcade visitors, friends, and strangers. Your responses shape each interaction and reveal the stories people are taking into the final evening.
- **Make small choices with personal meaning** — Help a child win a pink bunny, share toast with ducks or a neighbor, pass along a beloved plant, collect the last can for your Monster tower, and leave a wish beneath a cherry tree.
- **Play Plasteroids** — Challenge Kaito to a score attack in a playable arcade minigame with movement, shooting, asteroid waves, three lives, and a local high-score goal.
- **Interact with a living world** — Pet a dog, sit on a park bench to watch the sunset, turn on the television, browse your apartment, and discover humorous and heartfelt details throughout the neighborhood.
- **A warm, atmospheric soundtrack** — Original music, an in-world radio with multiple tracks, and contextual sound effects accompany the changing light and quiet end-of-day mood.

Solstice is designed to be completed in roughly one sitting. Take your time, talk to everyone you can, and choose what you want your last five minutes to mean.
